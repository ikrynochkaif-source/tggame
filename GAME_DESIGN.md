# AI-Creator Game Design

## Core Fantasy

The player grows from a chaotic beginner into a professional AI creator who can run a small content studio. The game is funny, but the economy is intentionally strict: no AI subscription, no content generation.

## Session Loop

1. Check the current trend and available resources.
2. Buy or maintain AI subscriptions.
3. Choose a content job.
4. Choose a strategy: fast, tasteful, or automated.
5. Spend energy and AI credits.
6. Receive money, hype, reputation, XP, and portfolio progress.
7. Every 7 days, pay subscription renewals and receive new credits.

## Main Resources

- Money: pays for subscriptions, upgrades, and agents.
- AI credits: required for every content job.
- Energy: limits how much the player can ship before resting.
- Hype: helps leaderboard score and viral growth.
- Reputation: drives career growth and reduces the feeling of being a random prompt spammer.
- Portfolio: permanent progress from successful projects.
- XP and level: unlock harder content and improve long-term status.

## Progression

Career rank is calculated from reputation, portfolio, level, and hype.

Ranks:

- Novice with a prompt
- Neural freelancer
- AI creator
- AI producer
- Mini studio
- Prompt Empire pro

The player starts with enough money to activate one basic subscription, but not enough to blindly buy every tool. This forces early choices.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions cost money weekly and grant AI credits on renewal.

- PromptBox Basic: unlocks posts and copywriting.
- Pixel Forge: unlocks visual carousels and brand packs.
- Motion Lab: unlocks AI video.
- Agent Cloud: unlocks automation and agents-heavy work.

If the player cannot pay a subscription renewal, that subscription is suspended. This prevents infinite generation and makes cash flow matter.

## Content Jobs

Each job has:

- Required subscription
- Minimum level
- Credit cost
- Energy cost
- Base payout
- Reputation gain
- Hype gain
- XP gain
- Risk

Harder content pays more, but consumes more credits, more energy, and has higher failure risk.

## Strategies

- Fast: more hype, higher risk, lower quality.
- Tasteful: more reputation, lower risk, higher quality.
- Automated: saves energy, works best when the player has stronger tools and agents.

## Upgrades

Player can spend money on skills:

- Prompting: reduces generation risk.
- Taste: improves quality and reputation.
- Marketing: increases payout and hype.
- Ops: reduces energy costs and operational risk.

Costs scale by level to avoid instant maxing.

## Agents

Agents unlock by career rank and require one-time payment.

- Brief Cleaner: reduces risk.
- Taste Director: improves quality.
- Sales Agent: improves payout.
- Ops Agent: reduces energy cost.

## Anti-Exploit Rules

- Content generation is blocked without the required subscription.
- Content generation is blocked without enough credits.
- Content generation is blocked without enough energy.
- Subscription renewals can suspend tools if cash is low.
- Upgrades and agents have scaling costs and rank gates.
- Leaderboard score includes durable progress, not only current cash.
